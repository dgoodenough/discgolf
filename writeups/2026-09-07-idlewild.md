(events_completed 20 → 21 MPO, 21 → 22 FPO). Odds are P(in the Powerball Cup

## MPO

* Nieminen (5th) is effectively done, 91% → 99.9%. Monn (T16) 75% → 86%. Mäkelä and Hebenheimer both went T6 and both jumped, 17% → 23% and 8% → 14% — enough to move Hebenheimer into the bubble group.
* Krans (T42) 82% → 65%, Williams (T35) 78% → 63%, Marwede (T24) 77% → 67%: all three finished the week on the rank they started it and lost anyway. Ellis (T44) is the only demotion, 19% → 14% and out of the bubble. Lizotte skipped, ended on exactly the number he started (13.6%), and slid four spots doing it.
* Two that look wrong and aren't: Villmann went T11 and dropped 53% → 50% — a T11 is what the model already had him down for — and Bryant went T6 and moved half a point, too far back for points to matter.

## FPO

Straight inversion of Worlds: in August the bubble gained by attrition, this week the chase pack had its best week of the year all at once.

* Everyone who played lost ground except Cox (4th); everyone who sat out gained. Midtlyng 83% → 76%, Klezberga 95% → 88%, Huynh 95% → 93%, against Chocek 37% → 41% and Pierce 89% → 91%. Chocek has now skipped Worlds and Idlewild and gained at both — 30% → 34% → 41%. Star Montgomery (39th) is the casualty, 13% → 7%.
* **Fajkus finished 2nd and her odds went down**, 19% → 17%; Burge finished 3rd, 35% → 30%. Idlewild was the last stop before the playoffs where a win by itself buys a Cup berth, and from that far out that was most of what the week was worth — Burge's whole drop is that branch closing, her points odds and her MVP odds both unchanged.

## Tiers remaining

G. Gurthie crept over the line. FPO is unchanged except Ringbom, who entered GMC.

week, still more than 2x anyone else (Tuominen, 27%, is next). But the two players

MVP spots, both got *worse* at it: Villmann 40% → 38% on points, Evan Smith 48% →
44%. Smith is already in on his winner's invite and can still take one of the four
