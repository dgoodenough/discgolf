# What Idlewild did

*Model state: pre-event snapshot 2026-09-04 vs. post-event snapshot 2026-09-07
(events_completed 20 → 21 MPO, 21 → 22 FPO). Odds are P(in the Powerball Cup
field); "auto-bid" is P(top 28 MPO / 18 FPO on points).*

## MPO

* Nieminen's 5th (97 pts) took him #23 → #21 and effectively ended it: 91% → 99.9%. Biggest gain in the division.
* Monn was the only bubble player who gained by playing — T16 (56 pts), held #24, 75% → 86%.
* Two T6s (82.8) did real work below the line. Mäkelä went #33 → #31, 17% → 23%, auto-bid 12% → 21%. Hebenheimer went #32 → #29, 8% → 14%, auto-bid 5% → 13% — enough to move him *into* the bubble group.
* Bryant also finished T6 and jumped #78 → #69, and it moved his Cup odds half a point (15.2% → 15.7%). He's too far back for points to matter; all 15% of it is the MVP path.
* Ulibarri's 4th (105) is the quiet one. He's been at 100% all year on his winner's invite, but this took his odds of getting in *on points* from 13% to 34% — which matters to everyone below him chasing an MVP bid.

* On the other side: Krans (T42) 82% → 65%, the biggest drop in MPO; Williams (T35) 78% → 63%; Marwede (T24) 77% → 67%. All three finished the week on exactly the rank they started it (#26, #25, #27) and lost 10–17 points of probability anyway.
* Ellis (T44) is the week's only demotion: 19% → 14%, #35 → #37, auto-bid 12% → 5%. He drops out of the bubble into the MVP group.
* Lizotte skipped it, ended the week on exactly the number he started it (13.6%), and slid #29 → #33 doing nothing. Auto-bid odds now 0.7%.
* Villmann is the odd one: T11, +71.5 pts, #40 → #36 — and 53% → 50%. A T11 is about what the model already had him down for, and he spent an event getting it.
* Klein went T13 and is flat (53% → 53%) while falling #28 → #30; Ulibarri and Hebenheimer went past him.

## FPO

Straight inversion of Worlds. In August the bubble gained by attrition. This week
the chase pack had its best week of the year all at once and the bubble paid for it.

* Every FPO contender who played lost ground except Cox (4th, 6.4% → 6.7%). Every one who sat out gained or held.
* The bubble: Midtlyng (22nd) 83% → 76%, Klezberga (28th) 95% → 88%, Huynh (20th) 95% → 93%.
* The skippers: Chocek 37% → 41%, Pierce 89% → 91%, Kristiansen Steen 34% → 35%. Chocek has now skipped Worlds and Idlewild back to back and gained at both — 30% → 34% → 41%.
* What did it: Fajkus 2nd, Burge 3rd, Cox and P. Kohut 4th, K. Hart 8th, Tõugjas-Männiste and J. Gurthie T10, A. Mandujano T12. The 18th-place bar moved up under everyone standing on it.
* The casualty is Star Montgomery, 39th: 13% → 7%, #20 → #22, auto-bid 10% → 2.5%.
* And the one nobody would guess: **Fajkus finished 2nd and her odds went down**, 19% → 17%. Burge finished 3rd and went 35% → 30%. Idlewild was the last stop before the playoffs where a win by itself buys a Cup berth, and for players that far outside the top 18 that ticket was most of what the week was worth. Burge's entire drop is that branch closing — her points odds and her MVP odds are both unchanged; she banked 115 points and the only thing that actually moved was the ~4% chance she'd win the thing outright.
